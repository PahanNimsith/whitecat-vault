◆ GAME OVERVIEW
Hollow Knight: Descent into Hallownest

Hollow Knight කියන්නේ ruined underground kingdom එකක ගැඹුරුම රහස් හොයන exploration-heavy Metroidvania එකක්. ඔබ control කරන්නේ speech නැති The Knight ව. Dirtmouth වලින් descent එක start වෙලා Forgotten Crossroads, City of Tears, Deepnest, Ancient Basin වගේ zones හරහා story, combat, platforming, and lore එක එකට බැඳෙන ගමනක් මෙතැන ගොඩනැගෙනවා.

English subtitle: A haunting action-platformer where exploration reveals a fallen civilization.

ගේම් එකේ tone එක dark කියලා එක පදයෙන් කියලා ඉවර කරන්න බෑ. මේ ලෝකය melancholy, mystery, danger, and wonder කියන feelings එකට දෙනවා. එක් side එකක quiet benches, sad piano themes, and abandoned streets තියෙනවා; අනෙක් side එකක frame-tight boss fights, risky corpse runs, and high-precision platforming sequences තියෙනවා. මේ contrast එක තමයි Hollow Knight එකේ emotional identity.

English subtitle: Hallownest balances silence and brutality with rare consistency.

Player progression pure stat grind එකක් නෙවෙයි. New movement tools (Mothwing Cloak, Mantis Claw, Crystal Heart, Monarch Wings), spell upgrades, charms, and map knowledge combine වෙලා ඔබේ freedom එක expand වෙනවා. First playthrough එකේ confusing වගේ දැනෙන routes, later stages වල interconnected mastery map එකක් වෙලා feel වෙනවා. මේකේ value එක story ending එකට විතරක් සීමා වෙන්නේ නෑ; route memory, combat expression, and lore decoding එකත් equally important.

◆ STORY & LORE
From The Radiance to the Final Vessel

Hallownest ඉස්සර Pale King රාජධානිය වන්නට පෙර, dream-light deity එකක් වන The Radiance මගින් Moth tribe සහ bugs world එක influence වුණා. Pale King (Wyrm form එකෙන් rebirth වෙලා) bugs වලට higher thought දුන්නාම civilization, architecture, and institutions හැදෙනවා. ඒ වෙනස්ම order එක සමඟ Radiance worship decay වෙලා, collective memory එකෙන් Radiance අමතක වෙන්න පටන් ගන්නවා.

English subtitle: Hallownest was born when intellect replaced dream obedience.

Radiance erased වීම accept නොකළ නිසා dream-space හරහා return වෙලා The Infection spread කරනවා. Orange corruption එක bugs minds distort කරලා violence, madness, and collapse create කරනවා. Pale King මේ threat එකට direct war solution එකකට යන්න බැරිව, Void-based containment plan එකක් build කරනවා. ඒකට source එක The Abyss; එතැනින් emotionless vessels create කරලා one perfect shell එකකට Radiance seal කරන්න උත්සාහ කරනවා.

English subtitle: Infection is not just disease; it is memory and will returning.

රජු තෝරාගත්ත vessel එක The Hollow Knight. Black Egg Temple seals හරහා containment temporary success වෙනවා. හැබැයි vessel purity imperfect නිසා Radiance slowly leak වෙලා kingdom නැවත ruin වෙනවා. ඉතිරි වන්නේ Dirtmouth මතකය, scattered NPC stories, and dead infrastructure. අපි control කරන The Knight කියන්නේ Abyss discarded sibling එකක්; spoken dialogue නැති, but willful action තියෙන character. මෙයාගේ journey එක revenge story එකක් නෙවෙයි, rather sacrifice cycle එක break කරන identity trial එකක්.

English subtitle: The Knight's path is about ending a failed system, not inheriting a throne.

◆ GAMEPLAY MECHANICS
Loop, Economy, and Difficulty Curve

Main loop එක clear structure එකක් follow කරනවා: explore new route, fight enemy clusters, collect Geo, unlock benches/stations, acquire ability, return to blocked paths, then challenge bosses. Death economy එක වැදගත් because Geo drop වෙලා Shade encounter එකක් create වෙනවා. recover fail වුණොත් resource loss permanent. මේ system එක tension maintain කරනවා even in routine traversal.

English subtitle: Progression in Hollow Knight is a risk-managed loop, not simple checkpoint running.

Progression layers parallel විදිහට ඉදිරියට යනවා. Movement upgrades map access open කරන අතර, Nail upgrades (Old to Pure) direct DPS scaling දෙයි. Charm Notches build complexity unlock කරයි. Soul Vessels සහ Mask upgrades sustain potential වැඩි කරයි. Merchant network එකෙන් මේ systems practical වෙන්නේ: Sly general utility, Salubra charms/notches, Nailsmith nail tiers. Geo spending priority mismanage කළොත් early-mid game pressure වැඩි වෙනවා.

English subtitle: Build freedom grows only when movement, gear, and economy are balanced.

Difficulty curve predictable tutorial pattern එකක් නෙවෙයි. Player power spikes mostly skill-based; timing, spacing, pogo control, invincibility frame awareness, and healing windows identify කරන sense එක critical. Late-game content (Trial of the Fool, Path of Pain, Pantheons, NKG) mechanical mastery demand කරනවා. මේ නිසා progression stat gated වගේ නෙවෙයි, execution gated experience එකක්.

English subtitle: The game tests understanding, not just level or gear.

✦ This game's most unique feature is ability-gated map recursion: every new movement skill redefines old rooms into new puzzle routes.
